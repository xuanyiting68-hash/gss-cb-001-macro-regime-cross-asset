# Public content layer

This directory is for evidence-linked PandaAI / Zhihu / Xiaohongshu / Douyin / WeChat educational content.

## Rule

A content claim may not be stronger than the research evidence behind it.

Before publishing a post, map it to an entry in `docs/EVIDENCE_LEDGER.csv`.

## Required claim checks

Where relevant, the content must state:

- sample and horizon;
- descriptive vs causal status;
- whether multiple-testing correction was passed;
- whether OOS was passed for a forecast claim;
- important limitations.

## Current content priorities

1. Why computational replication does not automatically validate inference.
2. Statistical significance vs economic significance vs trading value.
3. What 20D / 252-observation endpoint / max drawdown mean.
4. Why the 2015 and 2022 first-hike Gold paths differ.
5. Why realized Fed actions are not clean monetary-policy shocks.
6. Why energy pressure must distinguish crude from refined products.
7. Why “price is high, therefore short” is not a valid commodity-reversal rule.
8. How a research result moves from exploratory → audited → OOS → deployable.

A larger social-content pack should be imported only after a final claim/source audit.
