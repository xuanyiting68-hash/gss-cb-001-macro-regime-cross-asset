# Energy Reversal State Machine v2
Date: 2026-09-23
Status: PUBLIC RESEARCH PROTOCOL / NOT A TRADING SYSTEM

## Why v2

A crack-spread narrowing rule is not enough by itself.

If crude falls faster than refined products, a product-minus-crude spread can widen mechanically even while the whole energy complex is falling. A robust reversal framework therefore needs two distinct channels:

1. supply normalization;
2. demand destruction.

It also needs a false-relief veto.

## States

### S0 — Normal
No extreme energy pressure.

### S1 — Pressure Building
Candidate signs:
- crude momentum positive;
- refined-product prices rising;
- product spreads widening;
- inventories tightening;
- physical/refinery constraints active.

### S2 — Pressure Extreme
Energy-pressure composite enters a predeclared extreme historical range.

**S2 is not a short signal.**

### S3 — Initial Price Rollover
Crude begins to fall and at least two refined products stop rising or decline.

This is an early-warning state only.

### S4A — Supply Normalization Confirmed
Candidate confirmation:
- major product prices continue declining;
- inventories improve;
- refinery/shipping constraints ease;
- no new physical disruption appears.

### S4B — Demand Destruction Confirmed
Commodity declines are accompanied by sufficiently weak activity / throughput / financial conditions.

This channel is separate because crude can collapse faster than refined products.

### S3-VETO — False Relief
Reject the initial reversal if:
- product prices reaccelerate;
- inventories remain tight;
- refinery/shipping stress remains active;
- a new physical/geopolitical/weather shock appears.

### S5 — Reversal Mature
A confirmed normalization/destruction state persists and inflation pressure/expectations begin to soften.

## Required long-history test

Primary comparison:

**S4-confirmed events vs S3 events that later receive VETO**

Primary commodity outcomes:
- 1M / 3M / 6M / 12M return;
- maximum adverse excursion;
- time to trough;
- realized/downside volatility.

Secondary outcomes:
- Gold;
- broad USD;
- 2Y / 10Y / real yields;
- equities;
- household inflation and inflation expectations.

## Point-in-time rule

All thresholds must be estimated from prior history only.

Inventory/refinery data enter using public release timing where the forecast claim depends on availability.

No event may be relabeled after viewing its future return.

## Falsification

The framework fails if:
- confirmed states do not outperform veto states OOS;
- adverse excursion is not improved;
- results are dominated by one crisis;
- pre/post structural subperiods disagree strongly;
- realistic release lags destroy the result;
- simple price momentum performs just as well.
