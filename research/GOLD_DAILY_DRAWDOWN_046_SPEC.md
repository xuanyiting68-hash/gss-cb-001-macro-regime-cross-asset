User-requested historical windows: 1999-05-01 through 2000-06-30; 2022-02-01 through 2023-03-31. First hike markers are official FOMC 1999-06-30 and 2022-03-16. A separately disclosed stricter 2022 check is 2022-03-17 through 2023-03-16, the first year beginning with the next trading day. End-window dates are inclusive calendar dates; observations are trading sessions.

Primary window maximum drawdown: among daily *closes*, find the greatest peak before a later trough within each specified window; return trough close / peak close - 1. Drawdown duration = calendar and observed trading days from peak close to trough close. Recovery extends **beyond the specified drawdown window** if necessary: the first subsequent daily close at or above the original peak close, then an explicitly labeled sensitivity of five consecutive daily closes at or above it. A first cross does not prove that price stayed above the level. Separately report the intraday-high-to-subsequent-intraday-low wick statistic, without confusing it with closing-basis drawdown. Never mix Stooq spot OHLC with World Bank monthly means, LBMA PM fixes or GC futures.
