# P0-B / P0-C Public Status
Date: 2026-09-24

## P0-B — Point vs Trend

Status: **PRIMARY HYPOTHESES NOT SUPPORTED**

The public lesson is not “trend is useless.”

The lesson is:

> Smoothing a macro series does not automatically create economically meaningful predictive information.

One of the strongest trend coefficients was statistically detectable, but model explanatory power remained below the predeclared economic-significance gate.

Public claim boundary:

**statistical significance ≠ economic significance ≠ trading edge**

Do not generalize the null result beyond the tested feature definitions, horizons and specifications.

## P0-C — Fed × China / A-shares

Status: **QUARANTINED / HYPOTHESIS-GENERATING**

The exploratory Fed-only panel shows economically noticeable differences in average A-share forward returns across realized HIKE/HOLD/CUT meetings.

However:

- realized FOMC action is not an exogenous monetary-policy shock;
- the formal China predetermined-state layer is incomplete in the executed panel;
- multiple-testing correction does not confirm the exploratory screens.

Therefore the exploratory panel may motivate a future identified design, but it cannot support statements such as:

“Fed hikes cause A-shares to fall.”

The preferred next design is:

`identified Fed shock × predetermined China state → dynamic A-share response`

with target/path/information decomposition where feasible.
