# HICP-ENERGY-002 — Refined-Product Pass-Through × Household Inflation
Date: 2026-09-23
Status: PUBLIC-SAFE RESEARCH CLOSEOUT / MECHANISM CANDIDATE

## Question

Does an oil-price increase carry more household-inflation information when refined-product prices also rise relative to crude?

The research separates:

- upstream crude movement;
- downstream gasoline/distillate/jet pressure;
- household motor-fuel inflation;
- inflation compensation;
- rates / Gold / USD follow-through.

The product-minus-crude measures used in this phase are research proxies, not publication-grade location/grade-matched exchange crack spreads.

## Main result

When crude rose and refined-product pressure also widened, same-month household motor-fuel inflation and 5Y inflation compensation were stronger than in crude-up months without the same downstream amplification.

This supports a useful **pressure-monitor** interpretation.

It does not establish a structural causal chain and it does not establish a trading rule.

## OOS boundary

The same refined-product variables did not create a robust next-month forecasting edge across Motor Fuel CPI, headline CPI or the cross-asset outcomes screened in the pilot.

The key distinction is therefore:

**useful contemporaneous transmission information ≠ validated next-period alpha**

## Research implication

The energy block should not be reduced to:

`WTI → CPI → Fed`

A more useful public research architecture is:

`Physical shock → crude → refined-product tightness → household fuel pressure → inflation / expectations → Fed repricing → cross-asset risk path`

## Current evidence label

**MECHANISM CANDIDATE / PASS WITH BOUNDARIES / NOT A TRADING SIGNAL**
