# Academic-paper firewall

This public repository and the private academic-paper repository have different roles.

## Keep private

Do not mirror into this public repository:

- meeting-level exact staff EFFR assumptions;
- private/frozen FOMC reaction-function panels;
- private S6D.3 result sets and unpublished conditioning diagnostics;
- source files whose redistribution rights are uncertain;
- unpublished paper tables not cleared for release;
- private correspondence, credentials or personal information.

## Safe to reuse publicly

Research infrastructure may be reused:

- point-in-time discipline;
- publication-before-decision timing rules;
- source/provenance registries;
- evidence ledgers;
- multiple-testing policy;
- falsification logic;
- common-sample comparison discipline;
- negative-result preservation;
- descriptive / associational / causal language taxonomy.

## Safe public companion work

When independently sourced and clearly labeled, the public repo may contain:

- Fed-cycle return/drawdown/recovery research;
- public-market Gold / USD / rates / commodity diagnostics;
- household inflation and public energy-price research;
- commodity turning-point research;
- A-share descriptive research with identification caveats;
- PandaAI research dashboards;
- evidence-linked educational/social content.

If public work motivates a new academic hypothesis, it should enter the private paper only as a clearly labeled post-lock extension or future-paper idea. It must not silently alter frozen paper hypotheses, samples, regime boundaries or primary specifications.
